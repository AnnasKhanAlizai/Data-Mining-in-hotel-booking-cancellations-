# Data-Mining-in-hotel-booking-cancellations-
Data Mining applied in hotel booking cancellations analysis via Saas 

## Business problem
Find out the key factors which impact on booking cancellations and develop best predictive models which are capable to identify the risk factors of cancellations. The relation of different variables, which impacts the booking cancellation like customer behaviors, market segment etc. hotel decision maker can make the right decisions and can reduce loss and improve profit.

Our aim is to analyze the main factors which impacts the hotel booking cancellations with the help of data mining techniques. Our dataset consists of 26 different variables related to the hotel booking which are, Is Cancelled, Is Repeated Guest, Required Car Parking Spaces, Booking Changes, Arrival Date Day Of Month, Children, Days In Waiting List, ADR, Adults, Lead Time, Total Of Special Request, Previous Cancellation, Previous Booking NotCanelled, Stays In Weekend Nights, Arrival Date Week Number, Babies, Meal, Customer Type, Deposit Type, Market Segment, Distribution Channel, Reserved Room Type, Assigned Room Type, Arrival Room Type, Arrival Date Month, Arrival Date Year. When we did exploratory data analysis, we found that our data is imbalanced, skewed and have missing values. To fix these issues I used tree-based imputation and also applied log with base 10 and square root transformation. We did analysis with the help of four different variants of the Regression and Decision Tree models where we analyzed, is model performance improved after imputation, transformation and variable selection? With the help of ROC index, cumulative lift and matric, we analyze the model performance. Our champion model is regression with imputation because of high ROC value.  Our findings tell us that our variables like lead time, ADR and previous booking influenced a lot on booking cancellations. Our report analysis insights can help the hotel management to improve their profit and demand by taking actions on it.
